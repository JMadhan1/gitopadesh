He listens first

Speak or type. If your words are brief, Krishna reflects what he heard and asks one gentle question before he teaches.

One verse, chosen for you

Your words are matched against all 701 verses. The Sanskrit and transliteration come from the Gītā itself. The English, Hindi and Telugu meanings are translations; the Hindi and Telugu were prepared with AI and reviewed against the Sanskrit.

Three languages

English, हिंदी and తెలుగు. The shlokas are in Sanskrit, shown in Devanagari, or in Telugu script when Telugu is chosen.

About the maker

Hi, I’m Madhan. I build Gitopadesh as a quiet place to bring a hard question and hear the Gītā answer it. See my other projects and say hello on my portfolio.

jmadhan.me ↗

Gitopadesh offers reflections drawn from the Bhagavad Gītā. It is not medical, legal or mental-health advice. If you are in crisis, contact a local helpline or someone you trust now.

Your words are sent to Amazon Bedrock to write Krishna’s reply, and each sentence he speaks is sent to Microsoft’s speech service. Gitopadesh does not store them; your conversation history stays only in your browser. · Support · hello@jmadhan.me · jmadhan.me
