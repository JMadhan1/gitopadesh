    Valor, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from hate and vanity—these are the qualities of one who possesses the divine, O Arjuna!

  • Verse 4

    Hypocrisy, arrogance, conceit, anger, harshness, and ignorance — these belong to one who is born to the demoniac nature, O Partha.

  • Verse 5

    Godly qualities lead to liberation; godless ones lead to bondage. Do not be anxious, Prince! You have the Godly qualities.

  • Verse 6

    In this world there are two types of created beings — the divine and the demoniac. The divine has been described at length; now hear from Me about the demoniac, O Partha.

  • Verse 7

    The godless do not know how to act or renounce; they lack both purity and truth. They do not understand the right principles of conduct.

  • Verse 11

    Beset by immeasurable anxieties that end only with death, regarding the gratification of desires as the highest aim, and convinced that this is all there is.

  • Verse 12

    Caught in the toils of a hundred vain hopes, enslaved by passion and wrath, they accumulate hoards of unjust wealth, only to indulge their sensual desires.

    Today I have gained this; tomorrow I will fulfill another desire; this wealth is mine now, and the rest will soon be mine.

  • Verse 14

    That enemy has been slain by me, and I shall slay others as well. I am the lord, I am the enjoyer, I am successful, I am powerful and happy.

  • Verse 18

    Given over to egotism, force, arrogance, desire, and anger, these envious ones hate Me dwelling in their own and others' bodies.

  • Verse 19

    Those hateful, cruel, and inauspicious beings—the lowest of men in the cycles of worldly existence—I hurl them again and again into demoniac wombs alone.

  • Verse 20

    So, reborn, they spend life after life enveloped in delusion, and they never reach Me, O Prince, but degenerate into even lower forms of life.

  • Verse 21

    The gates of hell are three: lust, wrath, and avarice; they destroy the Self, so avoid them.

  • Verse 22

    O son of Kunti, a person who is freed from these three gates to darkness strives for the welfare of the soul, and thereby attains the supreme goal.

  • Verse 23

    But he who neglects the commands of the scriptures and follows the promptings of passion does not attain perfection, happiness, or the final goal.
