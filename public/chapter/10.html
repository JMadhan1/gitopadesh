Chapter 10: Vibhuti Yoga

Divine Glories · 42 verses

Ask Krishna about this chapter
  • Verse 1

    Lord Shri Krishna said: Now, O Prince! Listen to My supreme advice, which I give you for the sake of your welfare, for you are My beloved.

  • Verse 2

    Neither the hosts of gods nor the great sages know My origin, for I am indeed the source of all the gods and the great sages in every way.

  • Verse 3

    He who knows Me as the unborn, without beginning, the Lord of the universe, he, stripped of his delusions, becomes free from all conceivable sins.

  • Verse 4

    Intelligence, wisdom, non-delusion, forgiveness, truth, self-control, calmness, pleasure, pain, birth, death, fear, and fearlessness;

  • Verse 5

    Nonviolence, equanimity, contentment, austerity, charity, fame, and infamy — these diverse dispositions of beings arise from Me alone.

  • Verse 6

    The seven great sages, the four ancient ones, and likewise the Manus, endowed with My nature, were born from My mind — from them have come forth all these creatures in…

  • Verse 7

    He who truly knows this glory and yogic power of Mine becomes established in unwavering yoga — there is no doubt about this.

  • Verse 8

    I am the origin of all; from Me everything proceeds. Knowing this, the wise, endowed with deep devotion, worship Me.

  • Verse 9

    With their minds fixed on Me and their lives surrendered to Me, enlightening one another and always speaking of Me, they find contentment and delight.

  • Verse 10

    To those who are always devout and who worship Me with love, I give the power of discrimination that leads them to Me.

  • Verse 11

    Out of compassion for them alone, I, dwelling within their hearts, destroy the darkness born of ignorance with the radiant lamp of knowledge.

  • Verse 12

    Arjuna asked: Thou art the Supreme Spirit, the Eternal Home, the Holiest of Holies, the Eternal Divine Self, the Primal God, the Unborn, and Omnipresent.

  • Verse 13

    So have said the seers, the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa; and Thou Thyself also sayest it.

  • Verse 14

    I accept all this that You tell me as truth, O Keshava; for indeed, O Blessed Lord, neither the gods nor the demons know Your manifestation.

  • Verse 15

    You alone know Yourself, by the power of Your Self; You are the Supreme Spirit, the Source and Master of all being, the Lord of Lords, the Ruler of the Universe.

  • Verse 16

    You alone can describe completely Your divine manifestations, by which manifestations You pervade and abide in all these worlds.

  • Verse 17

    O Master! How can I, through constant meditation, come to know You? My Lord! What are Your various manifestations through which I can meditate on You?

  • Verse 18

    Tell me again, I pray, of Thy power and Thy glory in full; for I feel that I am never satisfied when I listen to Thy immortal words.

  • Verse 19

    Lord Shri Krishna replied: So be it, My beloved friend! I will unfold to you some of the chief aspects of My glory. There is no end to its full extent.

  • Verse 20

    O Gudakesha, I am the Self seated in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, the middle, and also the end of all beings.

  • Verse 21

    Among the Adityas I am Vishnu; among luminaries, the radiant sun; among the Maruts I am Marichi; and among the constellations I am the moon.

  • Verse 22

    Among the Vedas I am the Sama Veda; among the gods I am Vasava. Among the senses I am the mind, and among living beings I am consciousness.

  • Verse 23

    Among the Rudras I am Shankara, and among the Yakshas and Rakshasas I am the lord of wealth, Kubera. Among the Vasus I am Pavaka, the fire, and among the peaked…

  • Verse 24

    Among the priests, know, O Arjuna, that I am the priest Brihaspati; of generals, I am Skanda, the Commander-in-Chief, and of waters, I am the Ocean.

  • Verse 25

    Of the great seers, I am Bhrigu; of words, I am Om; of offerings, I am the silent prayer; among things immovable, I am the Himalayas.

  • Verse 26

    Of trees, I am the sacred fig tree; of divine seers, Narada; of heavenly singers, I am Chitraratha, their leader; and of sages, I am Kapila.

  • Verse 27

    Among horses, know Me to be Uchchaihshravas, born from the nectar; among lordly elephants, I am Airavata; and among men, I am the king.

  • Verse 28

    Among weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows I am Kamadhuk, the wish-fulfilling cow. I am Kandarpa, the god of love, among causes of procreation, and among serpents I…

  • Verse 29

    Among the Nagas I am Ananta, among water-beings I am Varuna, among the ancestors I am Aryama, and among those who enforce order I am Yama.

  • Verse 30

    Among the demons I am Prahlada, and among those that reckon I am time. Among animals I am the lion, and among birds I am Garuda.

  • Verse 31

    I am the wind among purifiers, the king Rama among warriors; I am the crocodile among fishes, and I am the Ganges among rivers.

  • Verse 32

    I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of creation; among sciences, I am the science of spirituality; I am the discussion among disputants.

  • Verse 33

    Among letters I am the letter "A," and among compound words I am the Dvandva. I alone am inexhaustible Time, and I am the Creator facing in every direction.

  • Verse 34

    I am all-devouring Death; I am the Origin of all that will happen; I am Fame, Fortune, Speech, Memory, Intellect, Constancy, and Forgiveness.

  • Verse 35

    Among the Sama hymns I am the Brihatsama; among meters I am the Gayatri. Of the months I am Margashirsha, and of the seasons I am the flower-bearing spring.

  • Verse 36

    I am the gambler's cheat and the splendor of the splendid; I am victory; I am effort; and I am the purity of the pure.

  • Verse 37

    Among the Vrishnis I am Vasudeva, among the Pandavas I am Dhananjaya; among the sages I am Vyasa, and among the poets I am the poet Ushana.

  • Verse 38

    I am the scepter of rulers, the strategy of conquerors, the silence of mystery, and the wisdom of the wise.

  • Verse 39

    I am the seed of all beings, O Arjuna! No creature, moving or unmoving, can live without Me.

  • Verse 40

    O Arjuna! The aspects of My divine life are endless; I have mentioned only a few as an example.

  • Verse 41

    Whatever is glorious, excellent, beautiful, and mighty, be assured that it comes from a fragment of My splendor.

  • Verse 42

    But what is the use of all these details to you, O Arjuna? I sustain this universe with only a small part of Myself.
