Bhagavad Gita Chapter 7, Verse 6

Knowledge and Realization

एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय। अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा

etad-yonīni bhūtāni sarvāṇītyupadhāraya ahaṁ kṛitsnasya jagataḥ prabhavaḥ pralayas tathā

What does Bhagavad Gita 7.6 mean?

Know that all beings have these two natures as their source. I am the origin and likewise the dissolution of the entire universe.

Word-by-word meaning
