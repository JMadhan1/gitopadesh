Bhagavad Gita Chapter 9, Verse 31

The Royal Secret

क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति। कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति

kṣhipraṁ bhavati dharmātmā śhaśhvach-chhāntiṁ nigachchhati kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśhyati

What does Bhagavad Gita 9.31 mean?

He quickly becomes a righteous soul and attains everlasting peace. O son of Kunti, proclaim boldly that My devotee never perishes.
