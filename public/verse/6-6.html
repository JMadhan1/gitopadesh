Bhagavad Gita Chapter 6, Verse 6

The Path of Meditation

बन्धुरात्माऽऽत्मनस्तस्य येनात्मैवात्मना जितः। अनात्मनस्तु शत्रुत्वे वर्तेतात्मैव शत्रुवत्

bandhur ātmātmanas tasya yenātmaivātmanā jitaḥ anātmanas tu śhatrutve vartetātmaiva śhatru-vat

What does Bhagavad Gita 6.6 mean?

For one who has conquered the self by the self, the self is a friend of the self; but for one whose self is unconquered, the self remains in enmity, acting like an enemy.

Word-by-word meaning
