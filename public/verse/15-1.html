Bhagavad Gita Chapter 15, Verse 1

The Supreme Person

श्री भगवानुवाचऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम्।छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित्

śhrī-bhagavān uvācha ūrdhva-mūlam adhaḥ-śhākham aśhvatthaṁ prāhur avyayam chhandānsi yasya parṇāni yas taṁ veda sa veda-vit

What does Bhagavad Gita 15.1 mean?

The Blessed Lord said: They speak of the imperishable Ashvattha tree, with its roots above and branches below, whose leaves are the Vedic hymns; he who knows it is a knower of the Vedas.
