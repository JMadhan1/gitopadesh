Bhagavad Gita Chapter 1, Verse 22

Arjuna's Dejection

यावदेतान्निरीक्षेऽहं योद्धुकामानवस्थितान्। कैर्मया सह योद्धव्यमस्मिन्रणसमुद्यमे

yāvadetān nirīkṣhe ’haṁ yoddhu-kāmān avasthitān kairmayā saha yoddhavyam asmin raṇa-samudyame

What does Bhagavad Gita 1.22 mean?

So that I may look upon these warriors arrayed here eager to fight, and know with whom I must do battle in this great combat.
