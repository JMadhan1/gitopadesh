Bhagavad Gita Chapter 2, Verse 30

The Eternal Self and Selfless Action

देही नित्यमवध्योऽयं देहे सर्वस्य भारत। तस्मात्सर्वाणि भूतानि न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि

dehī nityam avadhyo ’yaṁ dehe sarvasya bhārata tasmāt sarvāṇi bhūtāni na tvaṁ śhochitum arhasi

What does Bhagavad Gita 2.30 mean?

The embodied Self in everyone's body is always indestructible, O Bharata; therefore, you should not grieve for any living beings.

Word-by-word meaning
