Bhagavad Gita Chapter 11, Verse 39

The Universal Form

वायुर्यमोऽग्निर्वरुणः शशाङ्कः प्रजापतिस्त्वं प्रपितामहश्च। नमो नमस्तेऽस्तु सहस्रकृत्वः पुनश्च भूयोऽपि नमो नमस्ते

vāyur yamo ’gnir varuṇaḥ śhaśhāṅkaḥ prajāpatis tvaṁ prapitāmahaśh cha namo namas te ’stu sahasra-kṛitvaḥ punaśh cha bhūyo ’pi namo namas te

What does Bhagavad Gita 11.39 mean?

You are Vayu, Yama, Agni, Varuna, the Moon, Prajapati, and the great-grandfather of all. Salutations, salutations to You a thousand times, and again and yet again, salutations, salutations to You!
