Bhagavad Gita Chapter 2, Verse 61

The Eternal Self and Selfless Action

तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्परः। वशे हि यस्येन्द्रियाणि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

tāni sarvāṇi sanyamya yukta āsīta mat-paraḥ vaśhe hi yasyendriyāṇi tasya prajñā pratiṣhṭhitā

What does Bhagavad Gita 2.61 mean?

Restraining all of them, one should sit steadfast in yoga, devoted to Me as the supreme; for the wisdom of one whose senses are under control is firmly established.

Word-by-word meaning
