Bhagavad Gita Chapter 18, Verse 77

Liberation Through Surrender

तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः। विस्मयो मे महान् राजन् हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः

tach cha sansmṛitya saṁsmṛitya rūpam aty-adbhutaṁ hareḥ vismayo ye mahān rājan hṛiṣhyāmi cha punaḥ punaḥ

What does Bhagavad Gita 18.77 mean?

And remembering again and again that most wondrous form of Hari, great is my astonishment, O King, and I rejoice again and again.

Word-by-word meaning
