Bhagavad Gita Chapter 11, Verse 47

The Universal Form

श्री भगवानुवाच मया प्रसन्नेन तवार्जुनेदं रूपं परं दर्शितमात्मयोगात्। तेजोमयं विश्वमनन्तमाद्यं यन्मे त्वदन्येन न दृष्टपूर्वम्

śhrī-bhagavān uvācha mayā prasannena tavārjunedaṁ rūpaṁ paraṁ darśhitam ātma-yogāt tejo-mayaṁ viśhvam anantam ādyaṁ yan me tvad anyena na dṛiṣhṭa-pūrvam

What does Bhagavad Gita 11.47 mean?

The Blessed Lord said: Being pleased with you, O Arjuna, this supreme form has been shown to you by Me through My own yogic power — resplendent, cosmic, infinite, and primeval, which no one other than you has ever seen before.
